# The Seva Innovation Challenge 2026 submission

`margadarshak-seva-2026.pptx` is the challenge's own six-slide template, filled in.
Export it to PDF before uploading — the template asks for PDF under 10 MB.

```sh
python3 make_chair.py        # the chair art, in the template's navy and orange
python3 fill_template.py     # template.pptx -> margadarshak-seva-2026.pptx
```

`template.pptx` is the organisers' original, kept so the fill can be re-run and
so any change is visible as a diff against what they published.

## What was decided while filling it

**Nothing claims a cost multiple.** The comparison table on slide 3 compares
against an attendant pushing a chair, not against an imported autonomous unit,
because there is no quote for one. Get a written quote and that row becomes the
strongest line in the deck; until then it would be a number a judge can ask about
and we cannot answer.

**The demand figure is labelled an estimate on the slide itself.** Roughly 1 in
100 passengers against published traffic for the two Goa airports. The Evidence
box says so in as many words. A figure a judge disproves is worse than no figure.

**"Saving in cost / time" is 3x, labelled simulated.** It is the caravan ratio —
one attendant shepherding three chairs rather than pushing one — and the caption
says it has not been measured. The honest alternative was to leave the box empty.

**"Jobs or livelihoods" is 0.** The jobs question gets asked, and the true answer
is the good one: these are posts airports cannot fill today. The chair does the
walking, the attendant does the helping.

**One placeholder is left on purpose:** the qualification line on slide 6.
